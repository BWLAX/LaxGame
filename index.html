Rules Used

Youth rules, simplified for arcade play.

  • Five players per team with no goalies.
  • No opening draw; the Voyagers begin with possession.
  • After a goal, the defending team receives the ball for a clear.
  • The crease is protected and field players must remain outside it.
  • Poke checks can dislodge the ball; body checking is not included.
  • Out-of-bounds possession goes to the team that did not touch it last.
